PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy should be straightforward.

PostChars processes text locally in your browser. No account is required.

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Text that you enter

When you type or paste text into the tools, processing takes place in your browser. The application does not send your text to a PostChars application server. Files you export are generated locally by your browser. Content you choose to copy or share remains under your control.

Optional local draft storage

The main workspace offers an opt-in “Save drafts in this browser” setting. It is disabled by default. If enabled, drafts, word-count goals, reading-speed preferences and up to 10 manually saved versions per page are stored in this browser's local storage. This data is not synchronized, uploaded or backed up by the application. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to view it. Avoid enabling local storage for sensitive text on shared devices. You can disable the setting or clear site storage in your browser settings.

Analytics

Google Analytics is not configured in the default source release. The deployment operator may optionally add their own GA4 measurement ID in public/analytics-config.js. If enabled, the site loads Google Analytics only after a visitor chooses “Allow analytics” in the privacy preferences. Events are designed to record tool usage and interaction metadata, not the text entered, pasted, transformed or copied. Google may process technical information such as browser and device details, approximate location, page information and online identifiers under its own terms. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising and third-party integrations

Advertising integrations, publisher identifiers, ad-network scripts and ad frames are not included or enabled in this source release. A person deploying a fork who adds advertising or other third-party integrations is responsible for configuring appropriate consent controls, updating this policy and complying with applicable laws and provider terms.

Cookies and similar technologies

The optional analytics preference is stored in browser local storage under postchars-consent-v1 for up to 180 days. You can change or withdraw that choice using “Privacy choices” in the site footer. The application itself does not require advertising cookies. Hosting providers, CDNs and security services may process network request metadata under their own policies; consult the policies of the services configured by your deployment operator.

Infrastructure and service providers

The application is designed to run as a static website. The operator chooses the hosting, CDN, DNS, email and other infrastructure providers. Those providers may process connection information needed to deliver and protect the site under their own terms.

External links

The site may link to external services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Retention and your choices

The application does not maintain a database of text entered into its tools. You can clear the editor or close the page at any time. Local drafts remain in the browser only when you explicitly enable that feature.

Changes

The deployment operator should review and update this policy whenever the site's configuration, hosting providers or integrations change.

Contact

For questions about this deployment, use the contact details configured by its operator. The repository owner should update the contact address before deploying a fork.
