ABOUT POSTCHARS

Simple tools for better writing.

PostChars is an independently operated collection of free online text tools for writers, creators, students and social media teams.

Why we built it

Writing should not require switching between dozens of complicated services. PostChars offers more than thirty free browser-based text tools: platform-specific counters, X thread splitting, Unicode checks, word and syllable analytics, identifier formatting, local text extraction, version-aware writing and editing.

What you can do here

Use our character counter, word counter, X/Twitter post counter, Instagram caption counter plus Unicode inspection, list sorting, text cleanup and detailed writing statistics. The tools are free to access without registration.

Privacy-first approach

The text you type or paste is processed in your browser. PostChars does not upload that text to an application server. Optional draft saving uses browser-local storage only when you explicitly enable it. Optional analytics is disabled by default and can be enabled by a self-hosting operator with their own GA4 property, subject to consent. See our Privacy Policy.

Independent service

PostChars is not affiliated with X, Instagram, LinkedIn or the other platforms mentioned on this site. Platform limits are informational references and may change.
