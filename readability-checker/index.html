What does Flesch Reading Ease measure?

The Flesch Reading Ease formula uses average sentence length and syllables per word to estimate how easy English prose is to read. A higher score usually means simpler text under this metric, but technical writing, names, abbreviations and diverse audiences can make the estimate misleading.

The formula is 206.835 − 1.015 × (words / sentences) − 84.6 × (syllables / words). The result can occasionally go outside the familiar 0–100 range for unusual input. We calculate syllables with a small deterministic rule-based estimator, not a pronunciation dictionary.

What is the Flesch-Kincaid grade level?

The related Flesch-Kincaid formula estimates the equivalent United States school grade level for English-language reading difficulty. It is 0.39 × (words / sentences) + 11.8 × (syllables / words) − 15.59. The output is an estimate, not a classification of any individual reader's skills.

What does my Flesch Reading Ease score mean?

As a rough guide, these traditional bands describe how easy a passage may be for a typical English-language audience. They are not a test of a reader's ability and are less reliable on very short or highly technical drafts.

90–100Very easy
70–89Fairly easy to easy
60–69Standard

A Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level of about 8 suggests difficulty roughly comparable to English material often associated with US eighth grade. It does not mean every eighth-grade reader will understand the passage. The two scores use different scales; do not compare the numbers directly.

How to make English text more readable

  1. Paste at least a paragraph of complete English sentences for a less unstable sample.
  2. Identify sentences that combine too many ideas. Shorten them only when it improves meaning.
  3. Replace unnecessary jargon with familiar terms while keeping precise definitions.
  4. Read the draft aloud and ask a real reader whether the explanation is clear.

The formulas are documented in Microsoft's readability statistics guide. The underlying word, sentence and syllable counting rules can differ between applications, so do not expect identical numeric scores.

Limitations of automatic readability scores

This calculator is intended for English. It ignores words written entirely in other scripts, and abbreviations such as Dr. can confuse sentence detection. For text shorter than 30 English words, the tool marks scores as unstable. A reading-ease number cannot measure accuracy, inclusiveness, meaning or a person's comprehension.

Frequently asked questions

Is a high readability score always better?

No. A concise technical explanation may appropriately use specialist terminology. Match the language to the intended readers and preserve important meaning.

How do I check the reading level of my writing?

Paste a paragraph of English into the tool above. Check both the Flesch Reading Ease estimate (higher is generally easier) and the Flesch-Kincaid US grade estimate. Then review the average sentence length and revise unclear sentences without sacrificing accuracy.

Does Google rank pages by Flesch score?

Do not treat this score as a direct Google ranking target. It is an editing heuristic, not a search ranking forecast.

Does PostChars send the analyzed text anywhere?

No. The readability analysis runs in the browser and does not submit your text to a PostChars server.
